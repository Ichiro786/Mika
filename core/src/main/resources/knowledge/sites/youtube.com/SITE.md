## What it is
A video platform.

## Pages and URLs
- Search: `https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=<encoded terms>`.
- Video: `/watch?v=<id>`; channels use the channel link.

## Features
- **Videos**: search results show title, channel, duration, view count, publish time. On the video page you can expand the description and scroll down to load comments. Public videos need no sign-in.
- **Channels**: name, subscriber count, About description and latest videos.
- **Captions**: videos with captions let you get the caption text; captions of long videos are very long.

## Known behavior
- View and subscriber counts are often shown abbreviated, such as "12K" or "120K"; the exact number is in the details.
- Private, members-only, age-restricted or region-restricted videos show no content, which is different from "no search results".
- Failing to get captions may mean the video has none, is restricted, or a network problem.
