## What it is
Uber Eats is a food-delivery app, package `com.ubercab.eats`.

## Screens
Has Home/search, restaurant menus, Cart and Orders. Restaurant food and Grocery, Convenience, Retail and similar are separate channels.

## Features
- **Restaurants**: listed by delivery address; you can see name, rating, reviews, estimated delivery time, delivery fee, offers, open status and links, with Delivery and Pickup modes. A closed restaurant has no estimated delivery time.
- **Menu**: dish names, categories, prices in the original currency, options, sold-out status. Usually viewable without signing in.
- **Cart**: shows variants, quantities, minimum order, each fee, address and unit details, and the total. Add adds one portion per tap.
- **Orders**: Orders holds past orders and the delivery progress of current ones.

## Known behavior
- Restaurants and supermarket/retail items belong to different channels.
- A sign-in or verification step may appear.
