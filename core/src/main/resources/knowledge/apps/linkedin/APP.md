## What it is
LinkedIn is a professional-networking app, package `com.linkedin.android`.

## Known behavior
- When not signed in, the app opens on a welcome page: a carousel of intro cards, "Sign in with email", and "New to LinkedIn? Join now".
- The welcome page may first show Google's "Sign in to LinkedIn" bottom sheet ("Continue as ..."). That sheet belongs to Google services and its elements cannot be read; close it with the "×" at the top right.
